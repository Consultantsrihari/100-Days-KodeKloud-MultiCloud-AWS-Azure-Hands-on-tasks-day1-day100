# AWS AMI Interview Questions

### Q1: What is an AMI?
An Amazon Machine Image is a template used to create EC2 instances.

---

### Q2: Why create an AMI?
For backups, scaling, disaster recovery, and cloning environments.

---

### Q3: What happens during AMI creation?
Snapshots of attached volumes are created and bundled into an image.

---

### Q4: What does --no-reboot do?
Prevents the instance from rebooting during AMI creation.

---

### Q5: Can AMIs be shared across accounts?
Yes, AMIs can be shared or copied across regions and accounts.
